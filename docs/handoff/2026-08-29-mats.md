# Handoff — the mats arc, 2026-08-29

**Branch `mat-depth-pass`, five commits, nothing on master.** Everything
below is judged and tested but unmerged, deliberately: `felt` is not in
`BETA_SETTINGS`, so a new felt id reaches production the day it merges.

Started from a brainstorm (172 ideas, 11 families, artifact linked in the
session) after Joe's ask for roll-mat alternatives, with his three seeds:
real sand, dirt, and a magical one where dice float into place amid mist.

## What Joe decided

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| The atlas spends 99.7% of itself outside the walls | **Re-point it now** |
| Next build | **Silt, stage one** |
| Register — the roster drifted to near-black hard surfaces | **One of each, deliberately** |
| Where the ceremony declaration lives | **Held Breath instead** (off the felt) |
| How far the re-point goes | **The surround too** |
| Beat strength on dark cloths | **Per-theme depth** |
| The mat's back corners sit inside the fog | **Push fogNear past the mat** |
| Grain character | **Denser and quieter** |

One call was left to me and I took **seamless** for what lies beyond the
walls — it forecloses nothing, and Lampfall and The Cloth are both better
judged once there are two mats to look at, which "one of each" sets up.

## What shipped

1. `c1f450a` — the declaration was never on the mat; the cloth was never one cloth
2. `d6969f6` — the declare beat is told in light (Held Breath; mat text retired)
3. `5e1bb42` — no die sits in fog; each cloth takes the beat its own way
4. `4e593e9` — the atlas points at the mat (one tiled cloth, mottle on vertex colours)
5. `c7aeacb` — Silt, the first mat that is not felt

**The felt went from 12.8 to 205 px per world unit.** That number is the
spine of the whole arc: it is why the ceremony line could not be made
legible in place, why the grain averaged into flat colour, and why every
surface-detail idea in the brainstorm kept hitting the same ceiling
without naming it. Resolution belongs to the *tile*, not the plane, so
re-pointing cost less memory than the composite it replaced.

## Bugs found on the way, none of which had a check

* The ceremony declaration ran off the mat at **all three** zooms, fitted
  to 26 world units and seated at a constant `z +3.4` — both survivors of
  the 30-unit mat that C25 caught at the shelf. Rendered as `ATE OF ST`.
* Every player at a table saw a **different cloth**: the felt was the one
  atlas still drawn with bare `Math.random`.
* A zoom moved the mat and never refreshed the fog; once that was fixed,
  `applyZoom` refreshed it *between* writing the new mat and the new eye.
* Obsidian's beat depth drove the rim light to **−0.13**. A one-sided
  assertion (`rim < 0.4`) waved it through.
* The three felt-id lists had no guard. A felt missing from the server's
  allowlist works in solo and silently refuses to stick at a shared table.
* `feltPixel`'s world→pixel mapping survived the re-point returning bytes
  from one corner of the tile — a check that passes while proving nothing.

   The key stands 67 degrees above a horizontal floor and the mood lamp

   128px roughnessMap at repeat(1,1) on the mottle's own eighteen lobes,

## Watch

* **A rolling voice's filters are readable now.** `clothAudioInfo().live`
  publishes band, tilt, depth and level off the standing AudioParams, which
  closes the gap docs/AUDIO.md §8.1 names out loud. `audio-venue` ⑤ still
  makes the weaker claim about the venue's grind; strengthening it is now a
  small edit rather than a new instrument.
* ~~**`audio-phases` is flaky under load.**~~ **Diagnosed and fixed
  2026-08-29.** It was never load: the pour that leg scans is deliberately
  UNSEEDED (`commandRoll('6d6')`, because the claim is about a real pour
  through a real tower), so the film is new every run, and every few hundred
  dice one clears the rolling bar for a SINGLE frame on its way to a stop.
  Phase is derived from speed and the rough byte from contact, so that die
  legitimately has one without the other. `0 of 1 frames` was the assertion
  overreaching. It now asks only of windows five frames or wider, and says
  why. The general lesson is the one worth keeping: **an unseeded scenario is
  a slow fuzz test, and its assertions have to hold for every film, not for
  the one you watched.**
* **`feltPixel` had zero test users** before this arc, and its comment
  claimed it proved slot decals survive swaps — decals C25 had deleted. It
  has two users now.
* Piping a test run through `tail` masks the runner's exit code. A
  `100/101` was read as green that way; it was a real failure.
