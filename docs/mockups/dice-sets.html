Mockup notes

  • Thumbnails. These are flat SVG stand-ins built from a real face-on icosahedron projection (front face, three mid facets, six rim slivers) tinted from each set's body / numeral / edge and shaded from its material metalness/roughness. Ship version renders live d20 thumbnails off roadmap §6's offscreen pass — sequence roadmap §6 first (§4.3).
  • Entry points (§4.3): a small circular control bottom-left of the intent card, and beside your own row in the players panel.
    Joe you
  • Your dice → POST /api/style {set} → SSE player-styled; the set is player identity, so every client sees your rolls in it (§4.2). This group is a local override serialized as @set=<id> in the #g= codec (§1.5) — no wire call.
  • No per-roll set choice by design (§4.2): an extra decision inside the tension beat kills the beat. Hence exactly two scopes here.
  • Plain panel, no ornate frame — §5.1 rations ornament to the roll-moment card only. Gold is a hairline on the selected ring, well under the 3% budget.
  • Obsidian Shroud is pickable and fully numbered (graphite numerals) — a face-down regular can live in it and still roll dice the table can read. The eye-slash marks kinship: its numberless internal twin obsidian-blank is the shrouded-die material for hidden rolls, and reveal cross-fades that blank into the roller's real set — the numerals arriving is the reveal beat (§3.1, §4.4).
  • Effects (ember, nebula) preview as thumbnail behavior since the thumbnail is the only preview; both stop under prefers-reduced-motion. sound profiles cannot be shown statically.
  • No authoring at launch (§4.3's resolved position): extends is the format's inheritance key — used by the built-ins, and honored for hand-written localStorage diceSets records. When authoring ships it arrives as a single New set… tile appended to this same grid; none is drawn here, so the grid is exactly the ten launch sets.
